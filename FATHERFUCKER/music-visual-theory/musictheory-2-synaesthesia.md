# Music Theory 2: Synaesthesia & Cross-Modal Perception

## Core Finding
Sound and light are both frequency spectra. The human nervous system processes both through the same pattern-recognition mechanisms. Color hearing (synopsis) and sound vision (phonopsia) are documented neurological phenomena.

## Historical Cross-Modal Mappings
| Composer/Artist | Mapping | Source |
|-----------------|---------|--------|
| Scriabin | C=red, D=yellow, E=light blue, F=red-purple, G=orange, A=green, H=blue | Prometheus: Poem of Fire |
| Kandinsky | Flute=light blue, violin=green, fanfare=red, drums=cinnabar | Synesthetic painting |
| Rimsky-Korsakov | C=white, D=yellow, A=pink, F=green, F major=gray-green | Personal correspondence |
| Kneževič | Do=green, Re=red, Mi=green, Fa=indigo, So=red, La=yellow, Si=red | New music theory (1997) |

## Physical Basis
| Domain | Frequency Range | Percept |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| Sound | 20 Hz - 20 kHz | Pitch |
| Light | 390 THz - 750 THz | Color |
| Mapping | log(f_sound) → log(f_light) via octave equivalence | Cross-modal transfer |

## Art/Music Term Correspondence
| Term | Visual Meaning | Musical Meaning |
|------|---------------|----------------|
| Color | Hue of light/form | Tone color/timbre |
| Tone | Color tone/value | Pitch, duration |
| Composition | Arrangement in pictorial space | Notation of whole piece |
| Harmony | Coexistence of art units | Consonant chord connection |
| Rhythm | Repetition of visual elements | Temporal tone repetition |
