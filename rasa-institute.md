# Rasa Institute — Academic Press for Indian Philosophy & Consciousness Science

## What It Is

An independent academic press publishing short, rigorous, affordable books at the intersection of Indian philosophy and contemporary consciousness science. Named after Abhinavagupta's *rasa* — the distilled flavour of a state of consciousness.

**Website:** rasa.institute
**Founding Document:** Google Docs link

## Three Convictions

1. Indian philosophy contains some of the most sophisticated epistemology, philosophy of language, and philosophy of mind ever produced — almost entirely inaccessible to non-Sanskritists.
2. The question of consciousness is the central unsolved problem in contemporary science, and the Indian tradition has been investigating it with rigorous tools for over a thousand years.
3. The physical book still matters — form is a statement about the seriousness of content.

## Publishing Strategy

### The Flywheel

Following the model in magnum-opus/21-UNSAID.md: publish open access first, invite community review, print the revised edition after enough feedback.

```
TO published on Satsang (open access, v1.0)
  → Scholar community comments, suggests alternatives, debates
  → Revisions incorporated (v1.1, v1.2...)
  → After 6-12 months: genuinely peer-reviewed by the community
  → Print the "critical edition" with apparatus:
    - The final translation
    - Alternative readings with reasoning
    - Scholar comments and responses
    - Full provenance chain (the 7-pass log)
  → Print edition becomes canonical reference
  → Revenue funds next translation
```

### Copyright

Translations are automatically copyrightable as derivative works. The 7-pass DeepSeek pipeline produces original translation work. Registration (~$50 in India) makes enforcement easier but isn't required — the real protection is the **auditable provenance chain**. A pirated copy lacks the 7-pass log, alternative translations, confidence scores, and scholar apparatus. The provenance is the value.

1. Publish TO on Satsang (v1.0) — Day 1
2. Open community review period — Months 1-6
3. Incorporate revisions (v1.1, v1.2) — Ongoing

The TO is free and open access during the entire review period. The print edition is a premium object for those who want the physical book.
