## Contact-sheet findings

- Plotinian scenes maintain a vertical, diminishing, source-dependent grammar;
  Abhinavagupta scenes maintain a horizontal, reciprocal, luminosity-conserving
  grammar.
- Comparison scenes preserve both systems at once instead of turning the essay
  into alternating illustrations.
- Matter, contraction, desire, embodiment, aesthetics, and caution change the
  topology rather than only changing color.
- High-audio renders retain internal rings, mirror channels, and split seams
  without clipping.
- The synthesis cross and final coda preserve disagreement: violet descent and
  cyan reflection remain directionally independent.

## Revisions after render

- The first shared mirror contour became a many-petalled emblem and repeated too
  strongly across the contact sheet.
- Replaced it with a cleaner, slightly unstable lemniscate. This preserves
  reflexive self-display while reducing icon fatigue and making paired
  manifestation read as relation rather than ornament.
- Re-rendered mature, latent, and resolved sheets after the change.

## Intentional risk

The lemniscate recurs in scenes about Abhinavagupta because it is the conserved
mirror law opposite Plotinian descent. It is interrupted by procession tunnels,
matter fields, branch fans, ascent gates, woven difference, and aesthetic
resonance so it functions as a comparative invariant rather than a universal
scene template.
