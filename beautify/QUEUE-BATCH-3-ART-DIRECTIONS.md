# Queue Batch 3 — Generative Short-Film Art Directions

This batch contains 111 original fragment shaders across five essays. Each essay
is treated as a continuous generative short film for audiobook-style narration:
the conceptual sequence comes from the PIL source, while visual material,
geometry, camera, transformation, and emotional rhythm are newly authored.

## Advancement over the previous batches

The previous communication review established that every scene needs a readable
visual verb and causal topology. This batch keeps that standard but expands the
unit of design from the individual frame to the whole film:

- recurring matter changes meaning instead of merely repeating a logo;
- low and mature `u` states stage an argument through transformation;
- audio affects pressure, topology, focus, phase alignment, and growth—not only
  brightness;
- each pack uses a different spatial logic and generative material;
- visually spectacular moments are placed where the essay changes scale or
  reverses an assumption.

## Shared cinematic extension

`beautify-archive/lib/cinema.glsl` adds original reusable systems for projective
warps, complex-coordinate lenses, curl-flow fields, wave interference, caustics,
hexagonal crystallization, polygon and star SDFs, logarithmic spirals, temporal
echoes, soft beams, iridescence, bioglass color, and cinematic finishing.

The library uses the small-function compositional approach associated with
[LYGIA](https://lygia.xyz). Organic field construction, fBM, and domain warping
were informed by [The Book of Shaders](https://thebookofshaders.com), while
rule-based motion and repeated-agent thinking were informed by
[Generative Design](https://www.generative-gestaltung.de/2/). All shader
implementations and compositions are original.

## 1. Lawforge phase-transition cinema

`freedom-before-causality` — **21 visual functions**

Fluid possibility selects trajectories, repeated rhythm aligns them, and stable
law crystallizes as a hexagonal phase. The film does not oppose freedom and
order: the same gold current first chooses, then repeats, then becomes the
reliable cyan lattice. Habit appears as a deep attractor groove; novelty branches
without destroying the field; recognition reveals the lattice as frozen rhythm
rather than an alien prison.

Technical grammar: derivative-shaded scalar fields, analytical streamlines,
phase contours, crystal-growth fronts, branching graphs, attractor curves,
caustics, and projective reveal.

## 2. Volumetric pressure opera

`gods-under-pressure` — **20 visual functions**

Charged fog contracts into masks and total-field capture. Fear proliferates
structure, desire bends trajectories toward an absent center, wrath reorganizes
the whole field thermally, and the gate reverses the direction of the camera.
Sound later reorganizes the same pressure into cymatic order. Reintegration
preserves intensity while returning multiple powers to one field.

Technical grammar: volumetric gyroid fog, transmittance accumulation, metamorphic
SDF masks, polar tunnels, kaleidoscopic pressure mandalas, chromatic caustics,
cymatic interference, temporal slicing, and abstract ash landscapes.

## 3. Holographic bioglass memory

`memory-before-brains` — **19 visual functions**

A moving amber event leaves a spectral wake after its cause has gone. Tissue
becomes a field of translucent cells; hysteresis gives entry and exit different
paths; regeneration follows a latent target contour; a second event reveals a
cryptic future; reset sweeps inherited state away. The final scenes separate
biological trace from felt ownership without denying either.

Technical grammar: pseudo-volumetric glass membranes, Fresnel rims, temporal
echo integration, morphogenetic SDFs, hysteresis phase paths, epigenetic braids,
tensegrity networks, distributed tissue fields, and reconstructive particle
gathering.

## 4. Non-Euclidean localization optics

`reality-localizes-itself` — **27 visual functions**

A borderless point field accepts a center. The center becomes body, viewpoint,
attention cone, world model, predictive loop, biography, and defended self.
Objects freeze from action; language freezes flow into tokens; other minds
remain irreducible apertures within a shared field. Recognition widens the
viewpoint and reveals camera, cone, object, brain-image, and observer as events
inside the same curved manifold.

Technical grammar: projective and complex-plane lensing, warped world grids,
view cones, nested frames, sensory orbits, landmark constellations, predictive
loops, flow localization, multi-perspective portals, and curved-screen reveals.

## 5. Perceptual crystallization

`recognition-before-perception` — **24 visual functions**

The viewer first faces unstable spectral noise. One region gains thisness;
particles align into a figure; familiarity precedes the name; names compress
flow into tokens; categories impose basins. Illusion is shown as a positive
competing coherence, while recognition failures preserve features without a
whole. The final invariant sigil is not retrieved from elsewhere: it becomes
visible as the entire field reaches phase agreement.

Technical grammar: high-density particle Gestalt, moiré interference, Voronoi
thinking, phase-aligned contours, anamorphic sigils, ambiguous ribbons,
fragmented feature masks, prediction-error paths, category clusters, salience
fields, and global coherence.

## Review protocol and result

Every shader was:

1. statically audited for required uniforms and standalone structure;
2. compiled with `glslangValidator`;
3. rendered in a headless software WebGL 2 pipeline at `u = 0.72`;
4. reviewed as a complete pack contact sheet;
5. rendered again at low `u`, with different time and audio values;
6. revised where clipping, repetition, or literalism obscured the concept.

The five packs occupy clearly different visual worlds, but share a stronger
cinematic discipline: every transformation is tied to the essay’s claim.
Crystallization means law, mask capture means possession, spectral persistence
means memory, lens curvature means localization, and phase agreement means
recognition. The result is less like animated illustration and more like five
generative films that can carry narration on their own.
