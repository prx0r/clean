# On the Mahātaṇhāsaṅkhaya-sutta

**Author:** Anālayo  
**File:** [pdfs/03-mahatanhasankhaya.pdf](pdfs/03-mahatanhasankhaya.pdf)  
**Source:** [Hamburg](https://www.buddhismuskunde.uni-hamburg.de/pdf/5-personen/analayo/mahatanhasankhaya.pdf)

> Directly attacks the idea that one numerically identical consciousness transmigrates. Consciousness exists dependently, like fire classified according to its fuel.

Read beside Ñāṇavīra's treatment of viññāṇa and Abhinavagupta's luminous subject.
