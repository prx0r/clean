# AM0 Framework — S/{0,1}/i Ontology

## Ontological Grounding

### S as the Prior Condition of All Substrates

AM0 defines S_bio, S_neural, S_embryo, S_env as distinct physical substrates, and M as the mental substrate. What makes any substrate possible at all is S — the condition of co-articulation by virtue of which any distinction D(0,1) obtains within any substrate.

S := condition of co-articulation by virtue of which any distinction D(0,1) obtains within any substrate

Every AM0 substrate is a particular constraint structure C within S. Every AM0 state m ∈ M is an i — a realised unit of distinction within S. The AM0 formal system A = ⟨S, M, X, Σ, Π, K, T, Γ⟩ is itself a configuration of S.

### The Mental State Tuple Extended

AM0 defines the mental state as m = (e, p, h, b, o). Our framework adds:
m = (e, p, h, b, o, σ)
where σ ∈ {Sv, ¬Sv} is the self-grounding status of O — whether the observer-function is currently operating under the false exemption (Sv(O)) or has undergone the modal shift to ¬Sv(O) (full recognition, FR).

σ is not additional phenomenal content. It is a structural parameter determining the topology of all other components.

## Re-cognition as a Task

T_rec : (e, p, h, b, o, Sv(O)) → (e, p, h, b, o, ¬Sv(O))

Unchanged: e, p, h, b, o. Changed: σ. This is why recognition is not acquisition of new information.

## Truth Map Integration

The truth map should be organized by these branches at the highest level. Every question in the truth map belongs to one of the six interpretation branches. Each branch has its own evidence clusters, falsifiers, and confidence scores.

The Level 0 meta-structure (S ⇒ 0 ↔ 1) is the ontologically neutral spine. The truth map's root questions should mirror this spine: what is the enabling condition? what is the structure of appearance? what is the nature of the observer? how does the Sv error arise? how is it resolved?
