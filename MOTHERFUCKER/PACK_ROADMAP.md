High-Utility Pack Roadmap

Selection principle

Build packs around visual structures that recur across many essays, not aroundone philosopher, one chapter or one finished video.

The most valuable packs cover recurring explanatory jobs:

locating a process;

showing dependency;

comparing models;

moving across scale;

representing time;

distinguishing evidence from interpretation;

staging a practice;

preserving continuity through transformation.

Pack-building cadence

For each new essay:

Use existing packs first.

Record every missing visual capability.

Keep new work project-local during the first use.

Promote only assets reused twice or mechanisms that cover a verified gap.

Add provenance from the originating essays and PIL scenes.

Render a complete proof pack before allowing autonomous selection.

This turns essay production into library growth without allowing the libraryto fill with near-duplicates.
