## What v2 now enforces

1. A visual-free argument IR is completed before visual selection.
2. Every beat states claim, relation, source state, target state, and invariant.
3. Every mechanism declares which relations it can encode.
4. Argument roles constrain visual operators.
5. Every beat records concept-to-mark encodings and a motion proof.
6. Three candidates are considered; the winner and rejected alternatives are recorded.
7. Continuity systems have explicit introduction, development, and resolution.
8. Source ranges cover all units exactly once.
9. Estimated timing and exact narration timing cannot be silently mixed.
10. The auditor rejects incompatible geometry and reports structural repetition.
