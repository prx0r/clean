## Scene mapping

### 1. Citi Is Free

- Sanskrit: चितिः स्वतन्त्रा विश्वसिद्धिहेतुः॥१॥
- Transliteration: Citiḥ svatantrā viśvasiddhihetuḥ
- Working paraphrase: Citi is free and is the cause of the universe.
- Visual technique: harmonograph / progressive stroke

### 2. Universe on Its Own Canvas

- Sanskrit: स्वेच्छया स्वभित्तौ विश्वमुन्मीलयति॥२॥
- Transliteration: Svecchayā svabhittau viśvamunmīlayati
- Working paraphrase: By her own will, she unfolds the universe on her own ground.
- Visual technique: L-system / turtle growth

### 3. Knower and Known

- Sanskrit: तन्नानानुरूपग्राह्यग्राहकभेदात्॥३॥
- Transliteration: Tannānānurūpagrāhyagrāhakabhedāt
- Working paraphrase: Multiplicity appears as the differentiation of knower and known.
- Visual technique: coupled Lissajous curves

### 4. Contracted Universe-Body

- Sanskrit: चितिसङ्कोचात्मा चेतनोऽपि सङ्कुचितविश्वमयः॥४॥
- Transliteration: Citisaṅkocātmā cetano’pi saṅkucitaviśvamayaḥ
- Working paraphrase: The individual is contracted consciousness and a contracted universe-body.
- Visual technique: phyllotaxis point accretion

### 5. Consciousness Becomes Mind

- Sanskrit: चितिरेव चेतनपदादवरूढा चेत्यसङ्कोचिनी चित्तम्॥५॥
- Transliteration: Citireva cetanapadādavarūḍhā cetyasaṅkocinī cittam
- Working paraphrase: Citi descends and contracts into objects and mind.
- Visual technique: braided spline descent

### 6. The Māyā Perceiver

- Sanskrit: तन्मयो मायाप्रमाता॥६॥
- Transliteration: Tanmayo māyāpramātā
- Working paraphrase: The Māyā-bound perceiver is made of that contracted mind.
- Visual technique: Voronoi field with central blind spot

### 7. One Becomes Many

- Sanskrit: स चैको द्विरूपस्त्रिमयश्चतुरात्मा सप्तपञ्चकस्वभावः॥७॥
- Transliteration: Sa caiko dvirūpastrimayaścaturātmā saptapañcakasvabhāvaḥ
- Working paraphrase: The One appears as two, three, four, and seven groups of five.
- Visual technique: nested cyclic symmetry / combinatorics

### 8. Every Philosophy Is a Role

- Sanskrit: तद्भूमिकाः सर्वदर्शनस्थितयः॥८॥
- Transliteration: Tadbhūmikāḥ sarvadarśanasthitayaḥ
- Working paraphrase: Every philosophical position is a role played by consciousness.
- Visual technique: radial lens system

### 9. Three Coverings

- Sanskrit: चिद्वत्तच्छक्तिसङ्कोचान्मलावृतः संसारी॥९॥
- Transliteration: Cidvattacchaktisaṅkocānmalāvṛtaḥ saṁsārī
- Working paraphrase: Through contraction of power, the conscious Self becomes covered by impurity.
- Visual technique: moiré shells / constraint geometry

### 10. The Individual Performs Five Acts

- Sanskrit: तथापि तद्वत्पञ्चकृत्यानि करोति॥१०॥
- Transliteration: Tathāpi tadvatpañcakṛtyāni karoti
- Working paraphrase: Even while limited, the individual performs the five acts.
- Visual technique: pentagonal cycle

### 11. Five Acts Inside Perception

- Sanskrit: आभासनरक्तिविमर्शनबीजावस्थापनविलापनतस्तानि॥११॥
- Transliteration: Ābhāsanaraktivimarśanabījāvasthāpanavilāpanatastāni
- Working paraphrase: The five acts operate as manifestation, enjoyment, recognition, seeding, and dissolution.
- Visual technique: five-stage process diagram

### 12. Bewildered by One’s Own Powers

- Sanskrit: तदपरिज्ञाने स्वशक्तिभिर्व्यामोहितता संसारित्वम्॥१२॥
- Transliteration: Tadaparijñāne svaśaktibhirvyāmohitatā saṁsāritvam
- Working paraphrase: Bondage is bewilderment by one’s own powers through non-recognition.
- Visual technique: Clifford strange attractor

### 13. Mind Turns Inward

- Sanskrit: तत्परिज्ञाने चित्तमेवान्तर्मुखीभावेन चेतनापदाध्यारोहाच्चितिः॥१३॥
- Transliteration: Tatparijñāne cittamevāntarmukhībhāvena cetanāpadādhyārohāccitiḥ
- Working paraphrase: When this is recognized, mind turns inward and ascends to Citi.
- Visual technique: reverse flow-field convergence

### 14. Fire of Consciousness

- Sanskrit: चितिवह्निरवरोहपदे छन्नोऽपि मात्रया मेयेन्धनं प्लुष्यति॥१४॥
- Transliteration: Citivahniravarohapade channo’pi mātrayā meyendhanaṁ pluṣyati
- Working paraphrase: Even concealed, the fire of consciousness consumes the fuel of objects.
- Visual technique: Chladni nodal geometry

### 15. Universe Assimilated to Self

- Sanskrit: बललाभे विश्वमात्मसात्करोति॥१५॥
- Transliteration: Balalābhe viśvamātmasātkaroti
- Working paraphrase: When the power is gained, the universe is assimilated to the Self.
- Visual technique: Voronoi assimilation field

### 16. Liberation While Living

- Sanskrit: चिदानन्दलाभे देहादिषु चेत्यमानेष्वपि चिदैकात्म्यप्रतिपत्तिदार्ढ्यं जीवन्मुक्तिः॥१६॥
- Transliteration: Cidānandalābhe dehādiṣu cetyamāneṣvapi cidaikātmyapratipattidārḍhyaṁ jīvanmuktiḥ
- Working paraphrase: Liberation while living is firm identity with consciousness amid embodied experience.
- Visual technique: stable center / moving environment

### 17. The Middle Unfolds

- Sanskrit: मध्यविकासाच्चिदानन्दलाभः॥१७॥
- Transliteration: Madhyavikāsāccidānandalābhaḥ
- Working paraphrase: The bliss of consciousness is gained through unfolding the middle.
- Visual technique: coupled breath aperture

### 18. Four Means of Entry

- Sanskrit: विकल्पक्षयशक्तिसङ्कोचविकासवाहच्छेदद्यन्तकोटिनिभालनादय इहोपायाः॥१८॥
- Transliteration: Vikalpakṣayaśaktisaṅkocavikāsavāhacchedadyantakoṭinibhālanādaya ihopāyāḥ
- Working paraphrase: Means include dissolving thought, contracting and expanding power, interrupting breath-flow, and attending to endpoints.
- Visual technique: quadrant process visualization

### 19. Samādhi Becomes Continuous

- Sanskrit: समाधिसंस्कारवति व्युत्थाने भूयो भूयश्चिदैक्यामर्शान्नित्योदितसमाधिलाभः॥१९॥
- Transliteration: Samādhisaṁskāravati vyutthāne bhūyo bhūyaścidaikyāmarśānnityoditasamādhilābhaḥ
- Working paraphrase: Repeated recollection after samādhi yields continuously arisen samādhi.
- Visual technique: iterative trace accumulation

### 20. Sovereignty of the Wheel

- Sanskrit: तदा प्रकाशानन्दसारमहामन्त्रवीर्यात्मकपूर्णाहन्तावेशात्सदा सर्वसर्गसंहारकारिनिजसंविद्देवताचक्रेश्वरताप्राप्तिर्भवतीति शिवम्॥२०॥
- Transliteration: Tadā prakāśānandasāramahāmantravīryātmakapūrṇāhantāveśāt… iti śivam
- Working paraphrase: Entering full I-consciousness brings sovereignty over the wheel of one’s own powers. Thus: Śivam.
- Visual technique: radial deity wheel / white resolution
