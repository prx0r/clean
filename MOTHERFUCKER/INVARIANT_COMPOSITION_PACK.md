# Current Pack Review and Design Decision

## Current pack set

The framework now has three different kinds of pack:

### Domain implementations

- `human-anatomy`
- `yogic-subtle-body`
- `neurocognition`

These contribute actual assets and renderable mechanisms. Their strongest shared
advance is epistemic typing: anatomy, phenomenology, computational models and
contemplative maps are not silently collapsed.

### Formal inheritance roots

- `scientific-diagrams`
- `systems-dynamics`
- `temporal-processes`
- `embodied-phenomenology`
- `textual-scholarship`
- `tantric-cosmology`
- `comparative-epistemology`
- `data-visualization`

These are useful policy boundaries, but most are currently empty roots rather
than visual vocabularies.

### Kernel vocabulary

The built-in mechanisms cover limitation, point of view, attention, desire,
practice, reversal, systems, feedback, emergence and open questions.

## What was still missing

The framework could depict *what a domain contains* and *how a local mechanism
works*, but it lacked reusable mechanisms for the composition-level laws that
made the best films coherent:

- identity defined relationally rather than iconically;
- continuity governed by explicit conservation laws;
- path-dependent state and causal memory;
- value, velocity and acceleration separated;
- one modality leading while another lags;
- transitions carrying causal meaning;
- recognition designed as a perceptual transaction;
- climax distinguished from endpoint;
- an evaluator that rejects beautiful but interchangeable candidates.

`invariant-composition` fills that gap.

## Why this is not “Forest style”

The five-lobed seed appears in the demo because it is a clear test object. The
pack does not require forests, fugue, abundance, centered cosmograms, direct
address, silence or a particular recognition signature.

The reusable theorem is:

> Preserve a relational identity while changing its carriers, and make the
> audience learn to track the relation rather than the material instance.

Any child pack may replace the seed with:

- an interval sequence;
- a graph adjacency;
- a phoneme contour;
- a ritual relation;
- a biological morphology;
- a camera behavior;
- a topological defect;
- a textual syntax.

## Quality upgrade

Unlike the earlier packs, this pack includes a dedicated mathematical layer:

- similarity-transform invariant signatures;
- closed-path resampling;
- exponential causal memory;
- derivative-aware Hermite trajectories;
- parity and signed-area checks;
- topology and carrier geometry generators.

These operations support genuine semantic tests rather than decorative motion.
