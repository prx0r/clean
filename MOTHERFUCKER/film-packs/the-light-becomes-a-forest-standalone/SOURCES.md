# Sources and Epistemic Limits

## Scope

This is an artwork, not a scientific paper or a substitute for primary textual
study. Sources informed the distinctions in `essay.txt` and the artistic
mapping in `composition.json`. No source is treated as proving the
composition's metaphysical thesis.

biological agency proves a consciousness-first metaphysics.

## Trika, recognition, and reflexive awareness

- Abhinavagupta, **Parātrīśikā-vivaraṇa**, English translation and study by
  Jaideva Singh, archival scan:
  https://archive.org/details/abhinavagupta-para-trishika-vivarana-the-secret-of-tantric-mysticism-jaideva-singh

- David Peter Lawrence, **“Reflexive Awareness in the Trika Śaivism of
  Abhinavagupta”**, doctoral research:
  https://surajamrita.com/yoga/hidden/box1/Reflexive_Awareness-at-Trika-of-Abhinavagupta.pdf

- Mark S. G. Dyczkowski, **The Stanzas on Vibration** and research on Spanda
  and Trika, used as background for the brief
  `yasyonmeṣa-nimeṣābhyāṃ...` line in the narration.

- Jeffrey S. Lidke, **The Goddess Within and Beyond the Three Cities** and
  related research on Abhinavagupta and Trika, used as secondary historical
  orientation.

- Paul Eduardo Muller-Ortega, **The Triadic Heart of Śiva**, used as secondary
  orientation for heart, recognition, and nondual manifestation.

- Christopher Wallis, **The Recognition Sūtras**, used as a modern translation
  and interpretive aid, not as a replacement for historical scholarship.

The composition uses conventional tattva numbering for the referenced levels:
Śiva 36, Śakti 35, Sadāśiva 34, śuddhavidyā 32, māyā 31, kalā or limited agency
30, vidyā or limited knowledge 29, rāga 28, niyati 26, and puruṣa 25.
Transliteration is simplified in JSON identifiers. `kala` at level 30 means
limited agency and must not be confused with `kāla`, time, at level 27.

The artwork's mapping from tattva constraint to visual openness is invented for
this composition. It is not claimed as a historical Trika correspondence.

## Rasa

- **“Rasa Theory”**, Brill's *Encyclopedia of Hinduism*:
  https://referenceworks.brill.com/display/entries/ENHI/COM-2040070.xml

- **“Indian Rasa Theory”**, *Encyclopedia of Communication Theory*:
  https://sk.sagepub.com/ency/edvol/download/communicationtheory/chpt/indian-rasa-theory.pdf

Rasa informed the designed audience experience. Dominant and supporting rasas
do not function as measurements or fixed one-to-one color assignments.
`shringara` is used here in its broad relational and harmonizing sense; the
work contains no romantic narrative.

  https://lygia.xyz/

contract.

## Explicit non-claims

This composition does not claim:

- that active inference demonstrates Trika;
- that the six artistic controls measure consciousness or spirituality;
- that rasa has a scientifically fixed color, tempo, or geometry;
- that tattvas are brain regions or biological modules;
- that cells contain miniature human minds;
- that an ecosystem is literally one conscious organism;
- that every drive is wise or ethically good;
- that nonduality makes finite conflict unreal or every local perspective
  correct.

The bridge is compositional: both vocabularies can illuminate how limitation,
preference, relation, and scale alter the meaning of a self while retaining
different standards of evidence.
