# Perceptual and Emotional Review

## Review question

The primary metric was not technical novelty or shader density. It was:

> Can an audience feel the argument changing before narration names it?

The review therefore separated three tests:

1. **Silent legibility** — the stage action must read without voiceover.
2. **Musical causality** — counterpoint must reorganize the image, not decorate
   it.
3. **Emotional truth** — the rasa transition must arise from form, timing, and
   relation rather than a color label.

## Intended felt arc

The opening is spacious but not empty: quiet plenitude with the slight pressure
of something that could appear. Germination introduces wonder. Three powers
bring confidence and articulate motion. Constraint changes wonder into
tenderness for finite form. Metabolism and appetite make the work bodily and
urgent. Nested agency complicates the identity of the self. Conflict creates
genuine pressure without turning the whole world hostile. Reciprocity releases
that pressure into trust. The ecosystem movement feels exuberant because
independent acts fit without losing independence.

Recognition then removes musical leadership. During the five-second aperture,
the field remains present but unclaimed. The returning seed should register as
clarity rather than spectacle. The final forest does not feel like escape from
the body; it feels like ordinary locality re-entered with more room around it.

## Recognition sequence review

Five exact frames were inspected:

- **539.5 seconds:** the augmented gathering has nearly released its lines.
- **540.0 seconds:** musical features are zero; suspended visual traces remain.
- **542.5 seconds:** stillness holds without a blank or frozen impression.
- **545.0 seconds:** one large white-gold seed is unmistakable on the key line.
- **548.0 seconds:** the seed has opened immediately into plural forest life.

The first implementation failed the 545-second test. A generic crossfade had
already mixed the seed into the next scene. The transition was deferred and the
seed's body, contour, and local glow were strengthened. The revised frame makes
recognition the clearest object in the entire film, then relinquishes that
clarity to multiplicity three seconds later.

## Audio review

The generated MIDI has:

- format 1;
- seven tracks including conductor;
- 7,044 note-on events;
- 51 explicit subject entries;
- six musical voices;
- six or more catalogued subject transformations.

The orchestration avoids the previous failure mode of an undifferentiated
drone. Sustained instruments have bounded, voice-specific envelopes;
harpsichord carries articulated pulse; register and pan preserve independent
lines. Rhythmic density peaks in conflict, opens during care, gathers in
chorale, and restarts as contrapuntal abundance.

A rendered 538–549 second PCM excerpt was inspected sample-by-sample:

```text
538–540  non-zero cadence
540–545  exact zero
545–548  non-zero recognition return
548–549  non-zero forest continuation
```

The first sample at 545 is zero because the new envelope starts from a
click-free amplitude of zero; subsequent samples immediately carry the onset.
This is musically synchronous and avoids a digital discontinuity.

## Narration review

The initial narration was approximately 1,900 words and risked making the score
subordinate. It was reduced to roughly 1,560 words including headers. The final
script keeps:

- the central question;
- the prakāśa/vimarśa and svātantrya framework;
- will, knowing, and action;
- contraction and the kañcukas;
- allostasis and active inference;
- rāga as finite incompleteness;
- multiscale biological agency;
- conflict, care, and recognition;
- explicit separation between scientific models and Trika metaphysics.

Repeated descriptions of already-visible processes were removed. The text now
acts as attentional wind: it changes what the listener notices while the music
and animation retain formal leadership.

## Remaining aesthetic risks

No generative work has one definitive playback. The important controlled risks
are:

- The opening may appear very dark on poorly calibrated displays. This is
  intentional, but final mastering may lift exposure slightly rather than add
  more objects.
- High-resolution rendering reveals more grain and microstructure than the
  low-resolution review. Bloom should be rechecked after final color encoding.
- Procedural synthesis is deliberately otherworldly, not a substitute for a
  recorded orchestra. A future acoustic performance should preserve the MIDI
  timing and exact silence.
- Narration delivery can still overload the score if read too urgently. A calm
  performance with phrased rests is part of the composition.

## Final assessment

The finished system passes the central artistic test: the twelve movements are
not interchangeable scenes. Each performs the next necessary transformation of
the question, and the recurring seed/subject lets identity be recognized
through change. Its most successful passage is not the densest one. It is the
sequence from ecosystem through silence, returned seed, and plural forest,
where image, counterpoint, timing, and sentence make one argument without any
single medium explaining the others.
