# Perceptual and emotional review

## Outcome

The accepted film is not primarily serene. Its movement is:

```text
expectancy -> fascination -> intimacy -> playful inference ->
decisive naming -> inward unease -> organic comprehension ->
argument -> depth -> reversal -> quiet recognition -> shared wonder
```

The dominant aftertaste is that ordinary speech has become strange, tender,
and trustworthy again. The climax avoids a triumphal white flash. It gives the
viewer six seconds to inhabit meaning without a container, then lets the word
return as a precise copper event.

## Rejected first render

The first complete render was attractive but not yet sufficient. Recurrence,
the vowel chamber, and listener reversal already communicated strongly.
However:

- naming was a sparse thread with nearly invisible facets;
- inner speech collapsed into one arc and bright point;
- the sentence remained a chain of tiny marks;
- the semantic debate became muddy red noise;
- the speech levels were decorative lines;
- recognition was almost black;
- the final return resembled another boundary diagram.

The accepted revision changed causes, not just exposure:

- a visible moving front crystallizes a fluid field into cells;
- inner speech becomes a large self-folding phononic ribbon;
- sentence parts are embedded in one luminous moving organism;
- rival explanations become clean interpenetrating phase fields;
- four speech regimes gain distinct translucent material bodies;
- silence retains a slow violet standing wave independent of audio;
- the exact key sentence produces copper continuity and pearl focus;
- final glyphs migrate through, above, and below the thread.

## Exact recognition review

Five frames were inspected:

- `09:00.5`: cadence, with the last residual semantic weather;
- `09:01`: literal silence begins;
- `09:04`: the audio remains absent while a violet standing wave persists;
- `09:07`: copper and pearl appear at the first key-sentence syllable;
- `09:18`: full language returns as a field of crossings and glyph-flocks.

The MIDI validator parses note-on/note-off intervals rather than trusting a
manifest. Any note crossing `541.0–547.0` fails. The video can therefore remain
alive during silence without secretly inheriting an audio pulse.

## Failure conditions

Reject future revisions if:

- speech science and Trika metaphysics are presented as one evidential chain;
- Bhartṛhari is described as a neuroscientist or Abhinavagupta as accepting
  `sphoṭa`;
- the four speech levels become a four-row infographic;
- audio controls only final brightness;
- a caption is required to understand the visual action;
- every scene uses a centred ring, logo, or generic noise field;
- the sentence remains a diagram of connected tokens instead of one event;
- silence becomes black emptiness or the climax becomes a white flash;
- the final return implies that language or embodiment must be escaped.

## Render notes

The first full review rendered 36 WebGL2 frames: opening, mature, and transition
states for all twelve semantic intervals. After the first critique, revised
middle and return passages were rendered in isolated fresh WebGL2 contexts to
test the software-GPU instruction budget. A redundant layered pressure sample
in the naming passage initially caused a SwiftShader context timeout; it was
replaced with an analytic phase field. The final shader renders valid frames
and independently passes GLSL syntax compilation.
