# Prior Output Audit

Scope: 326 shaders across the first 15 queue essays, using the prior compile,
multi-state render, contact-sheet, and revision records.

## What worked best

| Pack / approach | Strongest result | Transferable lesson |
|---|---|---|
| `reality-localizes-itself` | attention cones, token freezing, shared target across viewpoints | causal topology can explain philosophy without labels |
| `memory-before-brains` | amber trace survives its event; hysteresis and reset alter response | persistence is clearest when cause and trace separate visibly |
| `gods-under-pressure` | pressure becomes mask, tunnel reversal, cymatic reintegration | atmosphere works when it changes agency and camera, not just mood |
| `freedom-before-causality` | fluid possibility repeatedly aligns and crystallizes as law | one material can explain emergence through irreversible state change |
| `recognition-before-perception` | audience experiences noise becoming thisness and coherence | phenomenology is strongest when the viewer undergoes the claim |
| `attention-creates-the-finite-self` | field is physically edited by salience, threat, and release | attention should alter reachable reality, not point at it |
| `body-knows-the-shape` | gold target state and dark morphospace clarify repair | counterfactual goals need a different material status from current form |
| `cells-that-solve-problems` | multiscale navigation and changing self-boundaries | scale transitions need explicit borders, goals, and reach |
| `body-electrical-society` | membrane, channels, and tissue consensus remain distinct | biological explanation improves when each scale has its own topology |
| `an-image-becomes-imaginal...` | image begins to answer rather than remain viewed | reciprocity is a camera and causality change, not a symbol |
| `a-temple-teaches-space...` | strongest depth, light shafts, embodied traversal | spatial philosophy benefits from traversable architecture |
| `a-ritual-object...` | convincing mineral material and internal history | a continuity object earns repetition when its material state develops |
| sacred mirror / name / dream | coherent optical, cymatic, and palimpsest identities | a strong visual language can hold disparate concepts together |

## What did not work

1. **Template prison.** Several early packs placed a new glyph in the same
   sparse nocturnal field. The images were elegant but the argument stopped
   moving.
2. **Central-object fatigue.** Relic, doorway, mask, portal, or body sometimes
   remained centered across too many modes.
3. **Stable camera fatigue.** The living temple achieved depth but overused its
   corridor view.
4. **Brightness as explanation.** Early pressure renders clipped to white; some
   scenes used bloom where a boundary or force relationship was needed.
5. **Literal figure failure.** Faces, hands, or bodies became cartoon diagrams
   when the material system was not strong enough. Geometric or field-based
   abstraction communicated better.
6. **Texture without hierarchy.** Structural-memory and some scalar-field modes
   became noisy enough to hide source, trace, or target.
7. **Continuity by repetition.** Reusing the same sigil, grid, portal, or tissue
   field did not automatically create a film. Continuity must preserve history
   while composition changes.
8. **Audio as exposure knob.** The weakest audio response only increased glow.
   The strongest changed phase agreement, pressure, flow reach, or growth.

## Best overall synthesis

The most reliable signature is the combination of:

- `reality-localizes-itself` for semantic topology;
- `memory-before-brains` for persistent material history;
- `gods-under-pressure` for dramatic spatial transformation;
- `freedom-before-causality` for state-change logic;
- `recognition-before-perception` for audience participation.

The final four films use that synthesis while deliberately changing their
surface worlds:

- whole-before-analysis becomes spectral coherence and analytical prism;
- regenerative memory becomes counterfactual anatomical optics;
- Plotinus/Abhinavagupta becomes paired infinity optics;
- lived time becomes a temporal exposure engine.
