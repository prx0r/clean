## M.1 — Knowledge Is Relational Structure

Every explanation borrows structure from prior explanations. Remove the prior structure and the explanation becomes unintelligible — not merely unjustified, but literally meaningless.

### Logical Structure

```
BECAUSE
Every explanation depends on prior explanations (Sellars 1956; Deutsch 2011)
FOLLOWS
Removing the prior makes the dependent claim lose meaning, not just justification
THEREFORE
Understanding requires grasping the support structure, not merely the conclusion
```

### Falsifier

A subject with verified zero prerequisite knowledge of domain D correctly explains — not merely states — a depth-3 claim in D, verified by blinded expert panel scoring on binary adequate/inadequate scale, N>20 replications.

### Source

Sellars 1956 §1-8; Popper 1959 §1-3; Deutsch 2011 Ch.1; Gödel 1931

## M.2 — The Starting-Point Problem Dissolves

You cannot search for what you know (no need) nor for what you do not know (you don't know what to look for). This is Plato's Meno paradox. The solution: you begin always in the middle — with inherited structure, which you then refine. There is no absolute starting point.

### Logical Structure

```
BECAUSE
You cannot seek what you do not know is missing (Plato Meno 80d-e)
FOLLOWS
A gap in support only becomes visible when the claim fails under a challenge
THEREFORE
We begin in the middle, not at a foundation
```

### Falsifier

A subject with undetected prerequisite gap G correctly identifies G as missing before encountering any claim that depends on G, verified by pre-test administered before any instruction, N>50.

### Source

Plato Meno 80d-e; Popper 1959 §1; Rumsfeld 2002

## M.3 — Explicit Dependency Mapping Resolves the Starting-Point Problem

If the support structure of a claim is fully declared, a learner can locate their position in it before attempting the claim. The gap becomes a named absence rather than an invisible void.

### Logical Structure

```
BECAUSE
If the support structure of a claim is declared, the learner can locate their position
FOLLOWS
The gap becomes a named absence rather than an invisible void
THEREFORE
Explicit structure is the mechanism by which M.2 is resolved
```

### Falsifier

Subjects given a fully declared dependency map perform statistically equivalent to subjects given no map on prerequisite-gap identification tasks, N>100, p<0.05, pre-registered.

### Source

Ausubel 1960; Deutsch 2011 Ch.1; Plato Meno — the paradox is resolved not by finding a starting point but by recognising that we always begin mid-structure

## M.4 — Falsifiability Marks the Boundary of Knowledge

A claim you cannot challenge is not knowledge but received authority. The limit of what you can state a falsifier for is the limit of what you genuinely know.

### Logical Structure

```
BECAUSE
A claim you cannot challenge is not knowledge but authority (Popper 1959)
FOLLOWS
The limit of what is genuinely known is the limit of what you can state a falsifier for
THEREFORE
Mapping falsifiers maps the actual boundary of knowledge
```

### Falsifier

A claim exists that is genuinely known — held with justified understanding verified by successful novel problem transfer — for which no falsifier can be stated even in principle by domain experts, N>3 independent expert panels.

### Source

Popper 1959 §4-6; Deutsch 2011 Ch.1

## M.5 — Explanations Are Scored by (Problems Solved) − (Unfalsifiable Claims Added)

An explanation that solves more problems is preferable. An explanation that adds unfalsifiable claims increases complexity without empirical content. The difference is a valid comparative metric.

### Logical Structure

```
BECAUSE
An explanation that solves more problems is preferable (Sober 2015)
FOLLOWS
An explanation that adds unfalsifiable claims increases complexity without content
THEREFORE
The difference is a valid comparative metric between explanations
```

### Falsifier

An explanation with lower score is unanimously preferred by domain experts over an explanation with higher score, where preference is based on epistemic quality not sociological factors, N>30 expert panels, p<0.01.

### Source

Deutsch 2011 Ch.1; Popper 1959; Sober 2015

## M.6 — The System Applies Reflexively Without Exemption

The meta-claims apply to themselves. The system has no privileged meta-status. Gödel's lesson is structural, not a gap.

### Application

M.1 applied to the knower → the knower is relational structure, not a foundation
M.2 applied to identity → the self begins in the middle, not at a starting point
M.3 applied to self-knowledge → the overlay covering what you are can be mapped
M.4 applied to self-inquiry → the boundary of self-knowledge is what you can falsify about yourself
M.5 applied to self-understanding → better explanations of the self solve more problems with fewer unfalsifiable claims

### Source

Gödel 1931; Nāgārjuna MMK 24.18; Rovelli relational QM

## The Final Synthesis

### 1. Reality is relation

No intrinsic properties. Every property exists only in relation to another system. Reached independently by: Nāgārjuna (pratītyasamutpāda = śūnyatā), Rovelli (no observer-independent state), Majid (dual structures, neither fundamental), Dharmakīrti (meaning through exclusion).

### 1.1 If reality is relation, there are no foundations

A foundation requires intrinsic existence. If nothing has intrinsic existence, the regress terminates not in bedrock but in coherence. The question "what is the absolute starting point?" assumes svabhāva. The assumption is false. The question dissolves.

### 1.2 The dissolution of foundations is not loss

It is the correct description of how knowledge actually works — a web of relational dependencies, not a chain terminating in self-evident axioms.

### 1.3 Hard problems have the same structure

Posit intrinsic existence → face inexplicable gap → declare "hard problem." The error is in the framing, not the phenomenon. The system catches this class of error before it crystallises.

### 1.4 The knower is subject to the same analysis

The knowing subject is relational structure, dependently arisen, empty of intrinsic foundations but conventionally real. This is not the end of the subject. It is the correct description of the subject.

### 1.5 The circle

The system opens with pratītyasamutpāda — dependent origination as the ground that dissolves the starting-point problem. It closes with Rovelli's relational QM — physical properties exist only in relation. These are the same structural claim, reached independently across 2000 years. The circle does not close on a foundation. It closes on itself — coherent, conventionally valid, ultimately empty, pointing at the same thing from both ends.
