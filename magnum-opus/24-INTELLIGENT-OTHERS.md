# Intelligent Others — Machine Consciousness, Hybrid Life, Astrobiology

## Identity
The frontier. AI consciousness, synthetic biology, exobiology, posthuman. Michael Levin's bioelectric networks, machine sentience, what counts as a self when the boundaries aren't biological.

## Guiding Principle
The most interesting philosophical questions of this century will be forced by science, not philosophy. Levin's work on bioelectric regeneration already challenges what we mean by "self." Machine consciousness will force the question harder. Astrobiology will force it differently. Intelligent Others is where I track these convergences.

## Content
- Michael Levin paper breakdowns (bioelectric networks, morphogenesis, distributed cognition)
- Machine consciousness debates (what would count as machine sentience?)
- Synthetic biology (hybrid life, chimeras, novel organisms)
- Astrobiology (what forms could life take? what counts as life?)
- Posthuman (where the boundaries dissolve)
- These are academic papers and talks that I want to understand and visualise
