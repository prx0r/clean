# Anakhya — Sanskrit Publishing

## Identity
The future Sanskrit brand. Academic publishing, translated texts, deep scholarship. The long game.

## Guiding Principle
Texts that have survived millennia deserve presentation that lasts centuries. Not clickbait. Not trends. The source material is the authority.

## Content
- Tantraloka translations (ongoing)
- Academic papers on Pratyabhijñā, Spanda, Trika
- Critical editions of Sanskrit texts
- Deep dives into specific concepts (kañcukas, tattvas, upāyas)
- Yogi documentaries grounded in textual scholarship

## Format
`logical-argument` for academic papers
`semantic-essay` for conceptual deep dives
Long-form, slow pacing, ivory manuscript aesthetic
EB Garamond + Noto Serif Devanagari

## Website
Digital library. Published texts, searchable glossary, cross-referenced translations.
The website is the primary artifact; video is secondary.

## Why It Exists
The content I want to read properly formatted and contextualised. Not commentary — primary sources with apparatus.
