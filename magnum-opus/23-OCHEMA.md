# Ochema — Comparative Metaphysics

## Identity
Sharp philosophy. Abhinavagupta vs Ñāṇananda. Neoplatonism vs Pratyabhijñā. Science vs metaphysics. Proofs, concept maps, structured arguments. No fluff.

## Guiding Principle
A philosophical position is only as strong as the objection it survives. Every video makes an argument, then stress-tests it. The viewer should come away knowing both the thesis and the strongest counterargument.

## Content
- Side-by-side comparisons (Abhinavagupta vs Ñāṇananda, Russellian monism vs Pratyabhijñā)
- Formal proofs (A1-A5 style premise chains)
- Concept maps (radial/hierarchy/chain layouts)
- Refutations and counter-refutations
- Debates between traditions

## Format
`argument-diagram` motif — claims, premises, refutations, branches, concept maps
Monochrome palette + status colors (red for refuted, green for resolved)
Source Serif 4 typography, crisp and scientific
Proofs, not narratives. The argument IS the content.

## Website
`ochema.xyz` — video library, argument maps, interactive concept graphs.
The argument is the artifact. Videos are proofs. Website is the map of all arguments.

## Why It Exists
The philosophical debates I want to see worked out rigorously. Not overviews — arguments with premises, objections, and resolutions.
