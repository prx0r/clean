# GreenScreen — The Conscious Content Platform

## Thesis

YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram are attention markets. Their incentive is engagement time, not human flourishing. The algorithms optimize for outrage, dopamine, and addiction because those maximize watch time, which maximizes ad revenue.

GreenScreen inverts this. The incentive is **value delivered**, not time spent. The algorithm optimizes for what the user explicitly wants, not what keeps them scrolling. The revenue goes to creators and causes, not shareholders.

## Core Principles

1. **Content must be educational, uplifting, or spiritually valuable.** No slop. No outrage bait. No misinformation. No algorithmic amplification of harm.
2. **80% of ad revenue goes to creators.** 20% funds the platform.
3. **Users control their algorithm.** They edit what content they see. No black box.
4. **Users allocate their attention budget.** Ad revenue generated from a user goes to causes/creators they pre-select.
5. **Live streams of real problem-solving.** Charity projects, free journalism, science in action.
6. **Advertisers are curated.** Only ads that align with the platform's values. No crypto scams, no fast fashion, no manipulative marketing.

## Why This Works (The Bet)

### The Creator Side

Current platforms take 45-55% of ad revenue. GreenScreen gives 80%. For a creator making $100k on YouTube, that's the difference between taking home $50k vs $80k. The 30% delta pulls creators who are already making educational/uplifting content.

Initial creators come from the existing educational/spiritual space (Academy of Ideas, Eternalised, Esoterica, ReligionForBreakfast, the entire philosophy/spirituality/documentary niche). They're already aligned with the values. GreenScreen just gives them better economics and a more ethical algorithm.

### The Consumer Side

There is a large and growing audience that:
- Is tired of outrage bait
- Wants to control what they see
- Cares about where their attention money goes
- Would pay a premium (or watch ads) for a feed they trust
- Values education, spirituality, and genuine content

This audience is currently underserved. YouTube's algorithm pushes them toward increasingly extreme content because that drives engagement. GreenScreen gives them exactly what they want — no algorithm manipulation, full transparency, value-aligned content.

### The Advertiser Side

Yes, no advertisers initially. That's fine. The platform builds an audience of conscious, intelligent, high-value consumers. When the audience reaches critical mass, advertisers pay a premium to reach them because:
- These users have disposable income
- They trust the platform
- They're actually paying attention (not doomscrolling)
- The brand safety is inherent (no adjacent outrage content)

GreenScreen curates its own ads — not programmatic garbage. Think: sustainable brands, educational services, ethical finance, meditation apps, book publishers, documentary filmmakers.

## Platform Architecture

### Content Moderation (The Green Filter)

```
Upload → Automated screening → Human review queue → Published
```

**Automated screening:**
- LLM-based content analysis against the GreenScreen values
- Checks for: educational value, spiritual value, uplifting value
- Flags: outrage bait, misinformation, hate speech, manipulative content
- First pass: DeepSeek/Claude evaluates against a rubric
- Second pass: community flagging system

**Human review:**
- For borderline cases, human reviewers decide
- Reviewers are community-elected (reputation-based)
- Clear appeal process for creators

**The rubric (what qualifies):**
- Educational: teaches a skill, explains a concept, provides context, deepens understanding
- Uplifting: inspires, motivates, provides genuine hope, showcases human achievement
- Spiritual: explores meaning, consciousness, ethics, philosophy, contemplative practice
- Documentary: honest portrayal of real-world issues, problems being solved, journalism
- Creative: genuine artistic expression that doesn't manipulate emotions

**Does not qualify:**
- Outrage bait, political tribalism, conspiracy theories
- Get-rich-quick, crypto/NFT promotion, manipulative marketing
- Reaction content, drama channels, hate-watching
- Misinformation, pseudoscience presented as science
- Anything designed primarily to provoke negative emotional responses for engagement

### The User-Controlled Algorithm

Users have a **feed builder** dashboard:

```
My Feed
├── Topics I want (checked)
│   ├── Philosophy / Consciousness
│   ├── Sanskrit Philology
│   ├── Documentary: Climate Solutions
│   ├── Meditation / Contemplative Practice
│   └── Ancient History
├── Topics I don't want (blocked)
│   ├── Politics (any)
│   ├── Celebrity News
│   └── Product Reviews
├── Creators I follow (prioritize)
│   ├── Esoterica (always show)
│   ├── ReligionForBreakfast (always show)
│   └── [custom list]
├── Content length
│   ├── Short (<10 min)
│   ├── Medium (10-30 min)
│   └── Long (30+ min)
└── Freshness
    ├── Last week
    ├── Last month
    └── All time
```

**No black box.** The algorithm is:
1. Filter by user's topic preferences (hard exclusion)
2. Prioritize followed creators
3. Score by quality metrics (completion rate, not click rate)
4. Mix in discovery from similar topics
5. Apply freshness filter
6. Return ranked feed

Users can export their algorithm settings and share them. "Here's my feed config — try it."

### The Attention Budget

Every user has an **attention budget** — the ad revenue generated from their views goes where they choose:

```
My Attention Budget
├── Default split
│   ├── 50% — Creators I watched (distributed by watch time)
│   ├── 30% — My chosen causes (charities, projects)
│   └── 20% — Platform sustainability
└── Custom allocation
    ├── Creators: 60% (distributed proportionally)
    ├── The Earth Fund: 20%
    ├── Free Journalism Fund: 10%
    ├── Sanskrit Text Preservation: 5%
    └── Platform: 5%
```

This gives users agency over where their economic value goes. It turns passive consumption into active allocation. Over time, users can see the impact: "My attention funded 10 hours of journalism this month."

### Advertising Model

**Phase 1 (0-100k users):** No ads. Platform runs on donations/grants/founder funding. Focus on building the creator and consumer base.

**Phase 2 (100k-1M users):** Curated ads only. Platform hand-selects advertisers that align with values:
- Educational services (MasterClass, Coursera, Brilliant)
- Sustainable/ethical brands (Patagonia, Allbirds, Who Gives a Crap)
- Book publishers (academic presses, philosophy publishers)
- Ethical finance (triodos, renewable energy funds)
- Meditation/wellness (non-predatory, evidence-based)
- Documentary film distributors

**Phase 3 (1M+ users):** Programmatic but constrained. Use an ad exchange but with strict brand blocklists and value-alignment scoring. Maintain the curated feel through aggressive filtering.

**The pitch to advertisers:** "These are the most engaged, intelligent, high-income viewers on the internet. They trust our platform. Your brand next to our content gets their genuine attention. You pay a premium, but you get premium results."

### Live Streams: Real Problem-Solving

This differentiates GreenScreen from every other platform. It's not just content about change — it's change happening live.

## Potential Issues & Mitigations

### 1. No Advertisers Initially

**The issue:** GreenScreen needs revenue to pay creators. Without advertisers, there's no 80% to distribute.

**Mitigation:** Phase 0-1 runs on grants/founder funding. Pay creators a smaller but guaranteed amount during the build phase. The advertising value proposition (conscious, high-income audience) is real — it just takes time to reach critical mass. This is a feature, not a bug: slow growth ensures content quality remains high.

### 2. Content Moderation at Scale

**The issue:** LLM-based moderation is expensive and imperfect. "Educational" and "uplifting" are subjective. The platform will face criticism for both over-moderation and under-moderation.

**Mitigation:** 
- Transparency in the rubric (public, versioned, debated)
- Community-elected review board for borderline cases
- Clear appeals process with independent arbitrators
- Start strict, loosen as the community demonstrates good judgment
- Accept that some good content will be rejected and some bad content will slip through — no moderation is perfect, and the cost of a false negative (missing good content) is lower than the cost of a false positive (amplifying bad content)

### 3. User Acquisition

**The issue:** Competing with YouTube is absurd. Network effects are brutal. Users won't switch until their favorite creators are there, and creators won't switch until the users are there.

**Mitigation:**
- Don't compete with YouTube. Compete with a **niche**. Start with the philosophy/spirituality/education community. That's already a large, underserved, high-value audience.
- The platform doesn't need 100M users to be successful. 1M passionate, engaged users with high attention quality is more valuable than 100M doomscrollers.
- Creators switch for better economics (80% vs 45%) and better audience quality. They don't need all their viewers to switch — they need enough to make it worth uploading. Cross-post to YouTube as a funnel to GreenScreen.
- Provide a migration tool: import your YouTube channel, subscribers, and video metadata in one click.

### 4. The Attention Budget Is Complex

**The issue:** Users won't configure their attention budget. It'll default to something and most people will never touch it.

**Mitigation:**
- Sensible defaults (50% to creators, 30% to causes, 20% to platform)
- Default causes are platform-selected (transparent, vetted, rotated)
- One-click "boost this creator" and "boost this cause" buttons
- Quarterly impact report: "Your attention funded X this quarter"
- Gamification: badges for configuring your budget, impact milestones

### 5. Algorithm Control Reduces Discovery

**The issue:** If users block topics, they don't discover content outside their filter bubble. The platform becomes an echo chamber.

**Mitigation:**
- Discovery mode: a separate feed tab that shows content outside your preferences, with a "why this was recommended" explanation
- Periodic "expand your horizons" prompts — "You watch a lot of philosophy. Try one documentary this week."
- Optional algorithmic boost: users can toggle "surprise me" for a percentage of their feed
- The filter bubble risk exists on every platform. GreenScreen at least makes the filter transparent and user-controlled, which is strictly better than a black box.

### 6. 80% Creator Split May Not Be Sustainable

**The issue:** YouTube's infrastructure costs are massive (bandwidth, storage, transcoding). 80% may not leave enough for platform operations, especially with a small user base.

**Mitigation:**
- Cloudflare Stream is significantly cheaper than YouTube's infrastructure (especially for video)
- R2 storage costs are minimal
- Workers compute is cheap at moderate scale
- The platform operates lean — no massive engineering team, no corporate overhead
- If 80% isn't sustainable, be transparent: "We're at 75% this quarter while we scale. Here's a breakdown of where the 5% went."

### 7. Legal Liability for Content

**The issue:** As a "curated" platform, GreenScreen may face higher legal liability for content moderation decisions than a "neutral" platform like YouTube.

**Mitigation:**
- Clear, public content guidelines
- Consistent enforcement track record
- Section 230 protections still apply in the US
- EU Digital Services Act compliance from day one (transparency reporting, user appeals)
- The "good actor" defense: a platform demonstrably trying to do good gets more benefit of the doubt from regulators

### 8. Creator Payment Infrastructure

**The issue:** Paying thousands of creators in dozens of countries is complex. Payment processing fees, currency conversion, tax reporting.

**Mitigation:**
- Use Stripe Connect or similar for multi-country payouts
- Batch payments monthly
- Minimum payout threshold ($50) to reduce transaction costs
- Transparent fee breakdown: "You earned $1,000. Fees were $30. You received $970."
- For creators in countries with limited payment infrastructure, offer crypto stablecoin payouts as an option

## What's Not Yet Solved (Open Questions)

1. **How to prevent gaming the "educational/uplifting" rubric?** Bad actors will eventually optimize for the rubric rather than the values. The rubric needs continuous adversarial testing.

2. **What happens at scale?** Moderation, support, payment infrastructure — all the boring operational stuff that breaks as you grow. The platform needs to be built lean but survive success.

3. **Who arbitrates values?** If the community disagrees about whether a piece of content is "educational" or "misinformation," who decides? The moderation council model works in theory but is hard in practice (see: Wikipedia, Reddit, every community-governed platform).

4. **Can it actually compete with YouTube?** Not head-to-head. But it doesn't need to. The question is whether the niche is large enough to sustain the platform. Philosophy + spirituality + education + documentary is a big niche, but it's not 2B users.

5. **What about comments?** Toxic comments destroy educational spaces. GreenScreen needs a fundamentally different comment system — one that's constructive by design, not reactive moderation of garbage.

## The Companion App: Birth Chart as User Profile

GreenScreen has a companion app built around the **birth chart** — not as a gimmick, but as the actual user profile, recommendation engine, and social connector.

### Why the Birth Chart Works as a Profile

Every user has a birth chart. It's deterministic (based on time/place of birth), unique, and meaningful within the spiritual/contemplative framework the platform serves. It replaces:

- **Username + bio** → The chart IS the profile. It says more about a person's temperament, strengths, and challenges than any written bio could.
- **Content recommendations** → People with similar chart placements tend to resonate with similar content. The algorithm learns: "Saturn in the 12th house users consistently watch contemplative practice content."
- **Social connections** → Connect with people who share your Moon sign, or your rising sign, or who have complementary charts.
- **Algorithm sharing** → "My feed is tuned by my chart. Share yours to see what resonates with your temperament."

### Features

**Profile & Discovery:**
- Birth chart as the primary user profile (public, private, or friends-only)

- Content recommendations tuned by chart resonance (users with strong 12th house = more contemplative content, strong 9th house = more philosophical content, etc.)

**Social:**
- Messaging with chart compatibility indicators (not "you're destined to be friends" but "your charts suggest complementary communication styles")

**Algorithm Publishing:**

- Feed lenses can be tuned by chart: "Optimized for Pisces Sun with Virgo Moon"

### Dating: Swipe by Philosophy, Not Appearance

Traditional dating apps optimize for photos and height. GreenScreen dating optimizes for **understanding**.

**How it works:**
- No photos in the primary swipe deck. Your "My Philosophy" page, your birth chart, and your favorite content are the profile.
- Match percentage is computed from: philosophical alignment (shared traditions, teachers, values), birth chart resonance, content preferences (what you watch, what you love), and communication style indicators.
- Icebreakers are built from shared content: "You also love the Spandakarika translation? What do you think of verse 2.3?"
- After matching, you can share your full attention budget, your algorithm config, your saved EOs — these become conversation.
- The goal is not endless swiping but **meaningful connection**. The system learns what kind of minds you resonate with and surfaces those people.

**Boundaries:**
- You can choose to show photos after matching, not before
- You control what data is visible at each stage (philosophy only → birth chart → favorite content → photos → contact)
- Harassment is structurally impossible because the matching is interest-based, not proximity-based
- The same moderation system (Green Filter) applies to all profiles and messages
