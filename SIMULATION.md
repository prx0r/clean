## Step 2: RO — Research Object (themed passages from each side)

From Josipovic (2019):
> "Nondual awareness can be described as a non-representational form of reflexive consciousness or consciousness-as-such, in which the usual subject-object structure of experience is absent while clarity of awareness is maintained or enhanced."

Josipovic distinguishes nondual awareness from:
- Mind-wandering (low clarity, high self-reference)
- Focused attention (high clarity, high subject-object structure)
- Open monitoring (moderate clarity, diminished self-reference but still intentional)
- Nondual awareness (high clarity, no subject-object, no intentional object)

From Metzinger (2020):
> "Minimal phenomenal experience (MPE) is the simplest form of conscious experience — a state of pure self-luminosity without any additional phenomenal content... MPE can be modelled as a self-model that has been reduced to its minimal functional core: the phenomenal representation of an epistemic subject."

From Laukkonen & Chandaria (2025):
> "Consciousness is the inevitable consequence of a system that models its own modelling — when the generative model infers itself as the cause of its own inferences, a self appears. Nondual states occur when precision weighting is globally reduced, collapsing the hierarchical self-model."

### Step 3b: Hardest Non-Equivalence

**Trika says awareness precedes structure. Science says structure produces awareness.**

This is the hard problem in microcosm. Trika starts from consciousness as given and asks how structure (world, body, self) appears within it. Cognitive science starts from structure (brain, prediction, model) and asks how consciousness appears from it.

The non-equivalence is not resolvable by evidence alone — it's a starting-point disagreement. But it generates **testable consequences**:

### Step 3c: Directional Critiques

**What modern science reveals about Trika:**
- Josipovic's 2014 data shows that nondual awareness has distinct neural correlates (default mode network decoupling + sensory network integration). If nondual awareness is the fundamental nature of reality, why does it correlate with specific brain states? Why does brain damage affect access to it?
- The fact that nondual states can be induced by psychedelics (psilocybin, LSD) suggests they may be a normal brain state, not an ontological revelation.

**What Trika reveals about modern science:**
- If nondual awareness is just a self-model collapse, why does it feel like recognition rather than loss? Self-model collapse should feel like confusion or depersonalization, not clarity. The phenomenology of nondual awareness is specifically one of *recognition*, not *deficit*.
- The hard problem remains: even if you model every correlate of nondual awareness, you haven't explained why any of it is *accompanied by experience*.

### Step 3d: Testable Consequences

From this tension point, we derive:

1. **Prediction**: If nondual awareness is just self-model collapse, then disrupting the default mode network should produce nondual phenomenology. This is testable with TMS. ✓ Supported by preliminary data. But does TMS produce *recognition* or just *confusion*? (Currently unresolved.)

2. **Prediction**: If Trika is correct, then nondual awareness should have no neural signature — it should be the absence of all signatures, not a specific signature. Josipovic's data shows a specific signature, which pressures Trika.

3. **Prediction**: Long-term nondual practitioners should show permanent changes in self-related processing, not just state changes. Preliminary evidence from Dor-Ziderman et al. (2016) shows trait changes in advanced meditators.

4. **What evidence would resolve this?**: A decisive experiment would need to distinguish between "the brain generating nondual awareness" and "the brain relaxing into a state that reveals what was already there." These are observationally equivalent under current methods. This is a hard problem.
